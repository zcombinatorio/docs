This section explains how to trade decision markets on Combinator.

## Getting Started

Each user gets a client-side wallet to execute trades and hold positions with. Once you log in, you should be able to access your wallet in the top-right of the header. From there, you can add funds by sending the asset you want to trade to the address.

## Enter a Market

In order to participate in a decision market, you need to enter a market with either SOL or the market's base asset. In this walkthrough, we will use `BASE` and `SOL` as examples.

- Entering a market with 100 `BASE` splits your asset into 100 `pass-BASE` and 100 `fail-BASE`
- Entering a market with 2 `SOL` splits your asset into 2 `pass-SOL` and 2 `fail-SOL`

## Trading

The Combinator frontend allows you to first choose whether you'd like to bet on the pass market or the fail market.

The **pass market** represents the price of the base asset if a proposal passes. The **fail market** represents the price of the base asset if a proposal fails to pass.

Trading on the pass market allows you to exchange `pass-BASE` for `pass-SOL` and vice versa. This only affects the pass market price.

Trading on the fail market allows you to exchange `fail-BASE` for `fail-SOL` and vice versa. This only affects the fail market price.

This allows you to express your belief on what the base asset price should be if a proposal passes or fails.

## Exiting a Market

If you have sufficient `BASE` or `SOL` on both the pass and fail markets, you're able to combine the assets and exit the market.

### What does that mean?

Let's say you have 100 `pass-BASE` and 80 `fail-BASE`. You're able to withdraw 80 `BASE` from the market. Afterwards you will be left with 80 `BASE` in your wallet, 0 `pass-BASE`, and 20 `fail-BASE`.

This also applies to `SOL`.

## Claiming

After the decision market resolves, you will be able to claim assets from the resolving market.

Let's say a proposal ended up failing and you have 50 `pass-BASE` and 75 `fail-BASE` as well as 2 `pass-SOL` and 1 `fail-SOL`.

This means you will be able to claim 75 `BASE` and 1 `SOL` from the fail market. The pass market assets are deemed worthless / non claimable.
